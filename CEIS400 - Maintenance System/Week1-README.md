## Overview

Week 1 established the full foundation of the project. The team identified the business problem, gathered requirements, wrote the initial system specification, and built out the project plan. All deliverables came from the case study provided by the professor describing GB Manufacturing's maintenance department problems.

The two core problems identified were:

1. The Equipment Depot was using a paper-based checkout system resulting in over $50,000 in lost equipment annually and checkout times of 2 to 10 minutes.
2. Employees only checked the Small Warehouse near the central office and ignored the Main Warehouse one mile away, causing excessive duplicate orders and carrying costs.

## Deliverables

### 1. Business Problem Scenario
**File:** CEIS400_Team6_BPS_Final.docx
**Prepared by:** Team 6

Covers the business problem and scope statement, project objectives, customers and stakeholders, project description, software engineering best practices, and major project deliverables. The MMMS was defined as a two-module system covering both the Equipment Depot and the Materials Warehouse problem.

Stakeholders identified:
- GB Manufacturing executive and operations team
- Maintenance Department Supervisors
- Maintenance Department Employees (carpenters, electricians, painters, welders, plumbers)
- Equipment Depot Staff
- Warehouse Personnel
- IT Department

### 2. Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
**File:** CEIS400_Team6_MMMS_SRS_Final.docx
**Format:** IEEE-830
**Prepared by:** Andres Marte

The SRS documents all functional and non-functional requirements for the MMMS. It is organized into 8 functional requirement groups with 43 individually numbered requirements.

Functional requirement groups:
- FR-1: Employee Authentication
- FR-2: Equipment Checkout
- FR-3: Equipment Return
- FR-4: Overdue Equipment Alerts
- FR-5: Materials Warehouse Search
- FR-6: Materials Order and Transfer Requests
- FR-7: Supervisor Dashboard
- FR-8: Offline Mode
